About me

8+ years building teams, systems & order

Today I'm the Human Resource Executive at GMI Interior & Fitout, a turnkey fit-out and interior design contractor working across the GCC on residential, office, retail, F&B, clinic and mall projects. A project-driven business lives or dies by its people pipeline — so I keep hiring, onboarding, documentation and labour compliance running cleanly behind every site.

Before Dubai, I spent three years with Associa Property Management, supporting the administration of 100+ strata properties — owner correspondence, records, receivables and the hundred small things that keep communities running.

At Walmart Canada I led a department and a team of 15, which is where I learned that scheduling, inventory and people management are really the same skill: seeing the whole system and making it easier for everyone inside it.
